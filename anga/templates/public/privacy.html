This is a portfolio project, so please use made-up details rather than your own.

What is stored

Anything you type into a form is stored in the site's database so the features can work: names, email addresses and booking details. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. For the demo card, only the last four digits are kept.

Emails

No email is ever sent. Confirmation messages are written to a simulated outbox that staff accounts can read.

Cookies

One functional cookie keeps you signed in and holds your language and currency choice. Your theme preference stays in your browser. There are no analytics or advertising trackers.
